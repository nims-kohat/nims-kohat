Diploma Programmes

NIMS Kohat offers 6 diploma programmes registered with the Faculty of Allied Health Sciences, KPK. These 2-year programs provide hands-on clinical training and job-ready skills in specialized healthcare fields.

🧪

Diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology (MLT)

⏱ 2 Years | Faculty of Allied Health Sciences KPK

This diploma trains students in clinical laboratory procedures, diagnostic testing and specimen analysis — essential for modern healthcare diagnostics.

📚 Subjects Covered

  • Hematology & Blood Banking
  • Clinical Biochemistry
  • Microbiology & Parasitology
  • Histopathology & Cytology
  • Lab Equipment Operation
  • Practical Lab Training

✅ Eligibility

  • F.Sc Pre-Medical or Matric Science
  • Minimum 45% marks
  • Male & Female both

🎯 Career Paths

  • Diagnostic Laboratories
  • Hospital Pathology Departments
  • Blood Banks
  • Research Institutes

Admissions 2025–2026

Admission Information

Review the eligibility criteria and apply today for session 2025–2026.

📋 Eligibility Criteria

  • F.Sc (Pre-Medical) or equivalent qualification from a recognized board
  • Minimum 50% marks in qualifying examination
  • Appearance in KMU-CAT (for BS programs) — Khyber Medical University Centralized Admission Test
  • Valid CNIC / B-Form of applicant
  • Domicile certificate of KP province
  • Certificates and mark sheets of all previous qualifications
  • Two passport-size photographs

📝 Programs & Duration

  • BS Nursing — 4 Years (KMU Affiliated)
  • BS Allied Health Sciences — 4 Years
    Specializations: Dental, Radiology, Anesthesia, MLT
  • Diploma in MLT — 2 Years
  • Diploma in Surgical Technology — 2 Years
  • Diploma in Radiology Technology — 2 Years
  • Diploma in Dental Technology — 2 Years
  • Diploma in Anaesthesia Technology — 2 Years
  • Diploma in Health Technology — 2 Years
  • All Diplomas registered with Faculty of Allied Health Sciences KPK
